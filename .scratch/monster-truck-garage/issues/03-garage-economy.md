# Garage economy: starter Trucks, Mods and Reward Tokens

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Map: [Monster Truck Garage](../map.md)

## Question

How does the Garage work as a system?

- Which starter Trucks can he choose from, how many are there, and can he switch later?
- What Mod categories exist (tires, body, paint, flames, lights, exhaust, horn…), how many Mods are in each, and what makes one "cooler"?
- What is the Reward Token (what it's called and looks like), and how many does a Round earn? Does a better performance earn more?
- How much does each Mod cost? How long should it take to afford the coolest Mod?
- How long is a Round, and what is the moment of reward at the end of one?
- Does anything depend on Level or Game Mode, for example Mods that only unlock at Level 3?

## Answer

Resolved by grilling (all recommendations accepted). Glossary updated: **Bolt** replaces "Reward Token"; new terms **Body** and **Slot**.

- **Round**: 5 correct answers (as today; a wrong answer only costs a retry). Earns a flat **3 Bolts**, with no performance bonus.
- **Bolt**: a chunky gold bolt icon, voiced as "You earned 3 Bolts!" Clearly not money. ⭐ stays as the in-Round progress marker.
- **Bodies**: 3 starter shapes (for example pickup, big-foot, dragster). He picks one on first launch and can switch free at any time. Unlocked Mods are shared across Bodies, and each Body remembers its fitted Mods. Every Mod must fit all 3 Bodies.
- **Slots**: 5 (Tires, Paint, Decals, Lights, Horn). Each Slot has a free default (plain paint, small tires, no decals, basic lights, basic beep). Horn is sound-only and can be tapped to hear.
- **Mods**: 3 per Slot, getting visibly cooler (bigger, shinier, glowing or animated), costing **3 / 9 / 18 Bolts**. That's 15 Mods and 150 Bolts in all: first Mod after 1 Round, the top rung after 6 Rounds, everything after about 50 Rounds.
- **No gating**: Level and Game Mode never lock a Mod; Bolts are the only way to unlock one.
- **End of Round**: the Bolts fly onto his pile with a clink each, then he gets two big buttons, **Garage** 🔧 and **Play again** ▶. If he can now afford something new, the Garage button pulses and the voice says "You can build something new!"
- **Goal**: by default it's the cheapest locked Mod; tapping any locked Mod makes it the goal. A small goal icon and progress bar shows during Rounds.
- **Unlocking**: one tap, fitted to the Truck straight away, final, with no confirm dialog and no refunds. Tapping a Mod he can't afford: the voice says "You need N more Bolts" and nothing is spent.
